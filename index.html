Rules of the Damned

  1. Respect the host, the home, and everybody at the party.
  2. Costumes encouraged. Main-character energy welcome.
  3. Don't touch what isn't yours. Haunted décor is not a souvenir.
  4. Ask before posting someone else's pictures.
  5. If you drink, pace yourself, have water, and arrange a safe ride home.
  6. Nobody has to drink or participate in any dare.
  7. Clean up after yourself. The host is not your personal cleanup crew.
  8. Keep the vibes good. We're here for Halloween, not a real-life horror story.
